Ledger Proof of Ownership

Prove ownership of your Pocket account to migrate to the new chain

Browser Not Supported

This tool requires WebUSB. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera. Firefox and Safari are not supported.

Secure Connection Required

This tool requires HTTPS. WebUSB and cryptographic verification will not work over an insecure HTTP connection.

Dev Mode

Using proxy: /speculos (proxied via serve.py to localhost:5005). Or enter a direct URL like http://localhost:5005

Instructions:

            
    
              
  1. Connect your Ledger device via USB
    2. 
              
  2. Unlock your Ledger and open the Pocket app
    3. 
              
  3. In the Pocket app settings, enable Blind Signing
    4. 
              
  4. Click "Connect Ledger" and select your device
    5. 
              
  5. Enter your account index (usually 0 for the first account)
    6. 
              
  6. Click "Prove Ownership" to sign a challenge message
    7. 
              
  7. Approve the blind sign request on your Ledger device
    8. 
              
  8. Export the proof JSON file and submit it below or send to the migration team
